 Maven Roasters Sales Analysis Dashboard

 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end sales analysis of Maven Roasters using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to analyze six months of transactional sales data, identify key business trends, and develop an interactive dashboard to support data-driven decision making.

The project includes data analysis, visualization, and business recommendations based on customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and operational trends.

Key Data Insights

Revenue Trend
- Revenue showed an overall upward trend from January to June, with the exception of a slight decline in February.
- The February decline was observed across all product categories, indicating an overall business slowdown rather than a category-specific issue.

Day-wise Performance
- Thursday, Friday, and Monday recorded the highest order volumes.
- A consistent decline in both revenue and orders was observed from Friday to Saturday across all three store locations.

Product Performance
- Coffee, Tea, and Bakery consistently emerged as the top-performing product categories.
- Coffee and Tea together contributed to more than **50%** of the total revenue.
- The Top 10 revenue-generating products were predominantly from the Coffee and Tea categories.

Hourly Sales Pattern
- Customer demand peaked between **7:00 AM and 11:00 AM**, with **10:00 AM** being the busiest hour.
- Order volumes declined after 11 AM and dropped significantly during the final operating hour.


Business Recommendations

Improve Saturday Performance
Investigate customer purchasing behavior on Saturdays and implement targeted marketing initiatives to improve weekend customer traffic and sales.

Leverage High-Performing Categories
Continue investing in Coffee and Tea through inventory planning, product innovation, and targeted promotions to maximize revenue.

Optimize Peak-Hour Operations
Ensure adequate staffing and inventory availability during the morning peak hours (7 AM – 11 AM) to improve operational efficiency and customer experience.

Increase Off-Peak Demand
Introduce promotional campaigns during afternoon hours to encourage customer traffic outside peak business periods.

Review Late Operating Hours
Evaluate the cost-effectiveness of operating during the final business hours and assess whether operational adjustments could improve overall profitability.

Improve Underperforming Products
Develop strategies to improve the performance of low-selling products through targeted promotions, bundling, or product positioning.
